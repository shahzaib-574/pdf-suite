Ream · PDF Suite

Privacy policy

Effective August 22, 2026

Ream processes the contents of your PDF, Word, and image files on your device. Ream does not upload document contents to its developer.

Documents and recent files

Files you select, generated documents, OCR results, and recent-file history stay in the app's local storage unless you choose to save or share them. Clearing recent files removes those locally stored copies. Ream has no user account or cloud document service.

Advertising

The Android app may use Google AdMob to display ads. Google Mobile Ads may process device identifiers, IP-derived general location, app interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals. Advertising requests are not sent until the applicable consent flow permits them. Where required, you can reopen Google's privacy choices from Settings.

Permissions

Ream uses the system file picker and share sheet. Camera scanning is started only when you tap Scan and uses the system capture experience. The app does not request broad access to your photo library or device storage.

Security and retention

Network traffic is restricted to encrypted connections. Local recent files remain until you clear them, remove the app, or the operating system clears app data. Files you export are retained wherever you choose to save them.

Your choices

  • Use tools offline when advertising services are unavailable.
  • Clear locally stored recent files from Settings.
  • Review or change ad privacy choices when the option is shown.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the Ream support repository.
