Ream · PDF Suite

Privacy policy

Effective August 22, 2026

Ream processes the contents of your PDF, Word, and image files on your device. Ream does not send document contents to the Ream developer, Google, or AdMob. A document leaves the app only when you deliberately save or share it to a destination you select.

Documents and recent files

Files you select, generated documents, OCR results, and recent-file history stay in the app's local storage. Ream has no user account or cloud document service. When you tap Save or Share, Android transfers the selected output to the document provider or app you choose; that destination handles the file under its own privacy terms. Ream does not send document contents, extracted text, filenames, or recent-file contents to its developer or to AdMob.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob to display adaptive banner ads on the Tools and Recents pages. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google through AdMob: approximate location inferred from the device's IP address; app interactions such as app launches, taps, and ad views; diagnostic information such as launch time, hang rate, and energy usage; and device or other identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google processes those categories for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Ream requests updated consent information before requesting an ad and requests ads only when Google's consent system says that it may. Depending on your region and choices, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, limited, or technical ads. Where required, you can reopen Google's privacy choices from Ream's Settings page.

Permissions

Ream uses the system file picker and share sheet. Camera scanning starts only when you open Scan: the app shows a live preview and may request the camera permission for that screen. You can also pick photos from the system gallery. The app does not request broad access to your photo library or device storage.

Security and retention

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit with Transport Layer Security (TLS), and Ream disables cleartext Android network traffic. Local recent files remain until you clear them, remove the app, or the operating system clears app data. Files you export are retained wherever you choose to save them.

Deletion and advertising controls

Clear Recents removes Ream's locally stored recent files and history. It does not delete advertising data already processed by Google. Ream has no account or cloud document record to delete. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in Android Settings under Privacy and Ads, use My Ad Center, and review how Google handles advertising data in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Use tools offline when advertising services are unavailable.
  • Clear locally stored recent files from Settings.
  • Review or change ad privacy choices when the option is shown.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the Ream support repository. Issues are public, so do not attach documents or include personal or sensitive information.
