Website privacy policy

Website only · Review draft updated 30 September 2026

Review draft — not a worldwide compliance certification. This notice describes the current website implementation. Hosting configuration, retention details and jurisdiction-specific obligations need review before public release. The draft date is not an effective date.

Ream is operated by shahdotdev, an independent developer in Pakistan. “Ream”, “we” and “us” refer to that individual developer, not a separate company. shahdotdev is the public developer name; any legally required full identity and contact details must be confirmed before publication. For support or privacy requests, email info@reampdfsuite.com.

This notice covers the browser website. The Android app has a separate privacy policy, including its advertising and app-update disclosures.

Documents stay in your browser

The current website processes selected PDF, DOCX and image files, generated files and extracted text locally on your device. It does not upload document contents, filenames, document passwords or recent-file history to the developer or a document-conversion server. PDF rendering, font assets and OCR workers and language files are served as website assets; downloading these assets is different from uploading your document.

Your browser may retain generated documents and recent-file metadata in IndexedDB so you can reopen them. The local library is limited to 200 items and 512 MB, subject to browser storage availability. A file chosen for a tool is not necessarily retained; generated results and files you save to the local library may be. Temporary processing data also exists in browser memory while a document is open. See Cookies & local storage for the storage inventory and clearing instructions.

Ream does not provide a cloud backup or account-based library. A shared browser profile may expose local files to other people using that profile. Export important files separately; browser storage can be cleared or evicted.

Website hosting and request information

Hostinger is the selected website hosting provider. Loading a hosted website sends ordinary technical request information to its hosting and any configured delivery or security services. This can include your IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, request time and referrer information. Such information may be used to deliver the website, diagnose faults, prevent abuse and maintain security. Local document processing does not mean that a hosted visit produces no technical data.

Host-side logging, enabled CDN/security features, hosting region, subprocessors, access controls and log retention have not yet been verified for the deployment account. These details must be checked and this notice updated before public release; no specific log-retention period or data-location guarantee is made in this draft. Hostinger publishes its privacy policy and data processing addendum. Those documents do not by themselves verify Ream's configuration or compliance.

Email, support and external services

If you email us, the message, sender address and any information you choose to include are processed to answer your request, provide support or handle a privacy enquiry. Email delivery involves your email provider and the receiving mailbox provider. Please do not send confidential documents, passwords or unnecessary personal information. If troubleshooting needs a sample, use a redacted sample and agree how it will be handled first.

When you download, save or share a document, the location or app you choose handles that copy under its own rules. Opening an external link contacts the destination site. These actions are separate from Ream's local processing. We do not currently operate website accounts, payments or a cloud document-upload service.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current website code does not set HTTP cookies or include advertising scripts, analytics trackers or third-party tracking pixels. It does use browser local storage for preferences and IndexedDB for local files. This statement concerns Ream's code, not unverified hosting-layer features. The Android app's AdMob advertising is not part of the website. We will review notice and consent requirements before adding non-essential tracking or changing document processing.

Retention and deletion

Local library copies remain in your browser profile until you delete them, clear the library, clear site data or the browser removes them. Delete an item in Your files, or use Preferences → Privacy & storage → Clear recent files, then confirm with Clear files to clear the current local library. Clearing the library does not reset appearance preferences or delete copies already downloaded, emailed, shared or saved elsewhere. Browser site-data controls can remove preferences and other stored site data.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve the enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. A specific mailbox retention schedule, its provider and deletion procedure still require operator review before publication. Request deletion by email; some records may need to be retained where the law requires it. Hosting log retention is also an outstanding deployment check described above.

Security and international access

Local files rely on your device and browser-profile security; the library is not separately encrypted by Ream. Password-protecting a PDF does not encrypt other local library copies. Use a trusted device, keep your browser updated and avoid shared profiles for sensitive documents. No system can guarantee absolute security.

The developer is in Pakistan and the website can be accessed internationally. Hosting and email infrastructure may process technical data or correspondence in other countries; the actual regions and any required transfer safeguards must be verified for the services used. This draft does not assert that every country has the same privacy rules or that all data remains in Pakistan.

Your choices and privacy rights

You control local library data through Ream and your browser. We cannot retrieve or remotely erase a local file that is not sent to us. For information we do receive, contact info@reampdfsuite.com to request access, correction or deletion, or ask a privacy question. We may ask for proportionate information to verify a request; do not send identity documents unless a secure, necessary process has been agreed.

Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to restrict processing, object, receive portable data, withdraw consent where processing actually relies on consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Rights, exceptions and response deadlines depend on the relevant law. This draft does not waive them or claim that EU, UK or US state privacy laws automatically apply to every visit.

Where a legal basis is required, operating and securing the website and answering ordinary enquiries may rely on legitimate interests, and responding to a service request may involve steps requested by you. The applicable bases, balancing assessments, notice details and any consent requirements must be confirmed against the actual deployment and processing before this draft becomes effective.

Young users and changes

Use the website only when you can lawfully do so. If your local law requires parental or guardian permission, obtain it. Do not send children's personal information in a support message. The developer must review age-related obligations for the audience and territories served; this draft is not a claim of COPPA or other children's-privacy compliance.

If the website adds uploads, accounts, payments, analytics or advertising, its disclosures and controls will need updating before the new processing starts. A final policy will show an effective date and material changes will be communicated appropriately. For an accessibility barrier or other concern, email the support address with the affected page and browser, without attaching sensitive documents.
